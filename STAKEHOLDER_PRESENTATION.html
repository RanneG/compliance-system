Industrial Compliance System (PTW)

Executive Briefing & Stakeholder Presentation Guide

Executive Summary: The Industrial Compliance System is a modern digital platform designed to manage the Permit to Work (PTW) lifecycle for critical healthcare and industrial equipment — including washer-disinfectors, endoscope reprocessors, autoclaves, and vacuum systems. It replaces slow paper permits with an instant mobile-first, QR-enabled workflow that ensures 100% safety compliance, photo evidence collection, and permanent audit readiness.

3. The 5-Step Digital Permit Journey

The platform guides every permit through a seamless, 5-stage lifecycle designed for speed, clarity, and safety:

1
Initiation Manager drafts permit details
2
QR Safety Check Engineer scans & signs safety rules
3
Maintenance Uploads photos & submits hand-back
4
Verification Dept head inspects & accepts fit
5
Vault Archive Manager audits & locks record
  1. Step 1: Permit Initiation (Manager): The manager drafts a digital permit selecting equipment serial number, hospital department, nature of work (Repair, Maintenance, Service), and decontamination confirmation.
  2. Step 2: Instant QR Share & Safety Check (External Engineer): The system generates a dynamic QR code. The field engineer scans the QR code with their mobile phone camera. No account creation or password is required. The engineer reviews safety rules and signs the declaration on screen.
  3. Step 3: Maintenance Execution & Photo Evidence (External Engineer): The engineer completes work, indicates whether equipment is Fit for Clinical Use, and attaches job photos or PDF certificates directly from their phone camera.
  4. Step 4: Clinical Verification & Hand-Back (Department Head): The clinical department head inspects the equipment, reviews the work summary and photo proof, and signs their digital acceptance.
  5. Step 5: Manager Audit & Permanent Vaulting (Manager): The manager performs final audit review. The permit is permanently locked in the searchable compliance vault.

4. Strategic Business Value & Return on Investment (ROI)

  • Zero Training Barriers for External Contractors: External service engineers do not need usernames or passwords. Scanning the QR code lets them complete safety declarations and hand-backs in under 2 minutes.
  • 100% Audit Readiness: During regulatory inspections, managers can search any equipment serial number and instantly retrieve full photo evidence, digital signatures, and historical timelines.
  • Reduced Equipment Downtime: Faster digital handovers mean critical medical washers, autoclaves, and reprocessors are returned to patient care sooner.
  • Regulatory Standards Built-In: Structured to satisfy healthcare engineering decontamination compliance standards (QA-ADM01 / EDUWI09).

5. Production Readiness & Security Highlights

  • Works on Any Device: Accessible from desktop PCs, iPads, tablets, and smartphones (iOS & Android).
  • Bank-Grade Data Protection: Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) with immutable UTC time-stamped signature logs.
  • Enterprise Cloud Ready: Deploys seamlessly into secure cloud environments (AWS, Azure, Docker containers) or on-premises hospital servers.
  • Single Sign-On (SSO) Integration: Managers log in securely using their existing organization credentials.
